Terms and house rules

How booking and payment work at The Don's Place.

Booking and payment

All bookings and payments are handled through our WhatsApp line. A booking is confirmed only after we reply and confirm payment on WhatsApp.

Check-in and check-out

We confirm check-in and check-out times with you on WhatsApp when you book.

Cancellations

We agree cancellation terms with you on WhatsApp before you pay.

House rules

  • Treat the apartment and furnishings with care.
  • Respect other guests and neighbours.
  • Park only in the parking space provided.

Management can update this page with the final policies.
